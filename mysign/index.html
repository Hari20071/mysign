How to make a good signature

  1. Keep it simple. Use your name or initials with 2-3 clear strokes. Complex signs are hard to repeat.
  2. Practise on paper. Write your chosen sign 20-30 times until your hand does it smoothly.
  3. Make it repeatable. A signature must look almost the same every time, especially for bank and legal documents.
  4. Slight slant and an underline give a confident look. Do not make it too big or too tiny.
  5. Use a smooth pen (gel or ball pen) and sign without lifting the pen too often.
